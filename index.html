ℹ️ How CelebTwin AI Works

CelebTwin AI analyzes your uploaded picture or webcam selfie directly in your browser using HTML5 Canvas pixel analytics and computer vision geometry:

  1. Skin & Bounding Detection: Maps your face center of mass and bounding box dimensions.
  2. Feature Profiling: Computes aspect ratio, bilateral symmetry across the facial midline, cheekbone prominence, jaw angle, and smile curvature.
  3. 12-Dimensional Vector: Normalizes your facial measurements into a multi-attribute vector.
  4. Cosine & Weighted Similarity: Compares your vector against our curated database of world-renowned celebrities to find your closest visual twin.

🔒 Privacy Assurance: Zero network uploads. All image processing occurs strictly on your machine in real time.
